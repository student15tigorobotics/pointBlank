Most Sketchfab options use CC-BY (attribution required) or custom licenses. Consider procedural generation or commissioning verified CC0 sources for Japanese architectural elements (oni red gates, sakura motifs, ronin themes).

## Summary

- **Audio:** 13 verified CC0-1.0 assets (4 ambience + 4 synthwave/cyberpunk + 5 SFX packs)  
- **3D Models:** 6 verified CC0-1.0 asset packs (Kenney: 5 sci-fi + OpenGameArt: 1 skybox collection)  
- **Textures & Artwork:** 3 verified CC0-1.0 mega-collections + 1 decal pack (5,840+ individual textures total)

**Top 3 Recommendations:**
1. **Kenney Audio + 3D Suite** — Most reliable, all CC0-1.0, GLTF/Godot 4 native, covers audio/UI/robots/structures
2. **PolyHaven Textures + HDRIs** — 850+ CC0 PBR textures + 990+ HDRIs; professional quality for neon sci-fi environments
3. **OpenGameArt Cyberpunk Music Pack** — 9 dedicated synthwave/cyberpunk tracks (T&T Free Cyberpunk Pack) + Space Synth Wave for neo-Tokyo aesthetic

All assets verified for license compliance on source pages. Ready for GPL v3 game integration.
